# LinkedIn Post — Whole Team Context Engineering Case Study

---

4 people. 1 keyboard. 1 AI.

James, Joel, Nechama, and I ran an ensemble where we put the AI in the driver's seat and learned to navigate together.

The team didn't need better tools.
They needed a better way to think.

So we built one.

We called it Whole Team Context Engineering — mob programming, but with AI as the driver and humans doing what humans do best: judgment, intent, direction.

What happened:

→ Solutions the team couldn't have reached alone
→ Faster throughput, less rework
→ Engineers who actually *enjoyed* the work more

The unlock wasn't the AI. It was the room.

If your team is sitting on AI tools they don't know how to use, I wrote about how we cracked it.
